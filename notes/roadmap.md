# Dragon Rider — Roadmap

## First milestone: playable dragon flight

Create a real Unreal Engine project and establish the rider, dragon, and initial environment foundations. Build toward:

1. A third-person warrior who can mount and dismount.
2. Dragon movement with steering, acceleration, momentum, and altitude control.
3. Climbing, diving, pull-ups, and a barrel roll or comparable maneuver.
4. Wing animation and a flight camera tuned for comfort and speed.
5. A small but visually ambitious Nordic landscape for high-altitude views.

## Later milestones

- Landing and takeoff.
- Dragon fire and fireballs.
- Combat and enemies.
- Larger connected world, missions, settlements, and story developed with the owner.

## Constraints

- Keep the final cinematic visual direction in view; staged development is a path toward that quality, not a change in ambition.
- Be mindful of 16 GB system RAM. Avoid large asset imports and expensive workflows until the local setup is understood.
- Make and document major architecture decisions with the owner before committing to them.
